About Me

I'm Chance Tower, a cybersecurity student at Full Sail University who already spends a lot of time making sure details are right. Right now I work as an Estimator at Clark Construction of Texas. I read civil drawings and specs closely, look for risk before it turns into a problem, and put together accurate bids on tight deadlines.

Before that, I spent years in roles where safety and procedure came first. As a correctional officer I enforced policy, wrote up incidents, and stayed calm under pressure. As a road specialist I verified field data and wrote reports on my own. That work taught me that good security comes from clear rules, careful documentation, and people who actually follow them.

I'm moving into cybersecurity because it brings those habits together with technology I've enjoyed since I started studying IT. I'm especially interested in governance, risk, and compliance (GRC), where I can help organizations understand their risks and protect the people who depend on them.

Background

I am a cybersecurity student based near Schertz, Texas, in the greater San Antonio area. I am drawn to cybersecurity because it combines technical problem-solving with protecting people and organizations. My interests include information security, risk management, governance, risk, and compliance (GRC), and network defense, and I am seeking an entry-level role as a SOC Analyst.

Education

I am completing a Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity at Full Sail University, with an expected graduation date of December 2026. I also earned an Associate of Science in Information Technology (2025) and a Computer Science Undergraduate Certificate (2023) from Full Sail. Through my coursework, including Project and Portfolio IV and V, I developed a complete security plan, wrote organizational security policies, assessed an organization's security maturity, and completed a PCI DSS Self-Assessment Questionnaire D (SAQ-D) for a fictitious company.

Applicable Past Work

Before and during my studies, I worked as an Estimator at Clark Construction of Texas, a Level 1B Road Specialist at Clark Asphalt Products, and a Correctional Officer with the Nacogdoches County Sheriff's Office. In those jobs I reviewed technical documents to find risk, verified data and wrote detailed reports, enforced security procedures and policy, and documented incidents. That experience strengthened my problem-solving, attention to detail, and composure under pressure, which I now apply to helping organizations protect their data and systems.
