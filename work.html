Work Example 1 · Project and Portfolio IV & V

Security Plan (Updated & Corrected)

This is version 5.0 of the security plan I helped create for SnowBe Online, a fictitious online retailer, in Project and Portfolio IV and updated in Project and Portfolio V. The plan sets a framework of policies and controls to protect SnowBe’s systems and data, including AWS-hosted systems, on-premises servers, sales-team VPN access, and retail locations in the United States and Europe, and it aligns with PCI DSS, NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5, GDPR, and SOX. In Project and Portfolio V, I added the Patch Management (PM-1), Security Maturity (SM-1), and System Development Life Cycle (SDLC-1) policies.

Collaborators: Caela Harris, Chance Tower, Gregory Borden, Will Harris, Angel Carrion, and Alex Marcum

Work Example 2 · Project and Portfolio IV

Security Policy 1: IR01 Incident Response Policy

This Incident Response Policy (IR01, version 1.0.1) establishes how SnowBe Online identifies, reports, classifies, and responds to security events and incidents. It assigns roles from executive leadership and an Incident Response Leader through IT, legal, and employees, and it requires a structured response lifecycle of detection, analysis, containment, removal, recovery, and after-action review. I wrote this policy as an individual project in Project and Portfolio IV, Week 1.

Collaborators: Individual project

Work Example 3 · Project and Portfolio IV

Security Policy 2: RA-01 Remote Access Policy

This Remote Access Policy (RA-01, version 1.0) sets the requirements for authorizing and controlling remote connections to SnowBe Online’s systems, including on-premises and AWS-hosted environments, in order to reduce the risks of remote access. It applies to employees, contractors, and third-party providers, requires that remote access occur only when authorized, and calls for encrypted VPNs and monitoring of remote traffic. I wrote this policy as an individual project in Project and Portfolio IV, Week 2.

Work Example 4 · CYB359

Simple Maturity Spreadsheet & Prioritization

This individual project for CYB359 scores SnowBe Online’s cybersecurity practices across the 17 domains of the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) 1.0 framework and ranks those domains by priority. Foundational technical controls come first, then visibility and monitoring, then response and recovery. The write-up recommends next practices in configuration management, identification and authentication, system and information integrity, and maintenance.

Collaborators: Individual project, by Chance Tower

Work Example 5 · Project and Portfolio I · 5:17

Marconi Law Firm's Network Modernization (Case Study)

A 5-minute recorded presentation proposing a network modernization for the Marconi Law Firm. Project scope, in my words from the presentation:

  • Centralize the business network.
  • Provide adequate technology with growth in mind.
  • Improve internal communication and collaboration.
  • Enhance productivity and security.

Solution:

  • business-class ThinkPad laptops
  • an HPE ProLiant DL360 Gen10 server with HPE storage
  • Ubiquiti/UniFi router, switch and Wi-Fi 7 access points
  • VoIP and conference phones
  • a conference room smart TV
  • Windows Server 2019 Standard and Windows 11 Pro
  • a network diagram and itemized cost sheet
